I'm Matt Booth. Software developer by day, amateur astrophotographer by night (literally, it only works at night).

This site is where I share what I'm learning about deep-sky imaging: the gear, the processing, the mistakes, and the occasional frame worth keeping. I'm based in the UK, so I spend a lot of time waiting for clear skies and not nearly enough time actually imaging.

## About the name

**Photon** + **Booth** = *the Photon Booth*. A photo booth, but for photons.

You sit in a photo booth, hold still, and a few seconds later a strip of slightly unflattering pictures falls out. Astrophotography is the same deal, except you hold still for six hours, the subject is 1,300 light-years away, and the pictures come out unflattering *after* three evenings of processing.

And yes, my surname is Booth. Sorry.

## What you'll find here

- **Exposures**: write-ups of individual targets, with the kit, settings and processing steps.
- **Lessons learned**: the things I wish someone had told me before I stacked 120 frames with a dust bunny in every one.
- **The odd detour**: occasionally something with no photons involved at all. Usually beer.
