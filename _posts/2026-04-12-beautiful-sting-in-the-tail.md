Had *ridiculously* good sky last night. This is an unfinished stack of what the Canon chucked out. Scorpio is at the bottom right of the Milky Way.

![An unfinished wide-field stack of the Milky Way, with the Scorpius region at lower right](/assets/images/beautiful-sting-in-the-tail/milky-way-scorpius.webp)

You may think it cost a fortune to create shots like this. But a 50-quid DSLR and about the same for the lens will do the job.

The camera wasn't even on a tripod. It was on the polystyrene tray from the boot of the crapmobile hire car, sat on the roof.

And even if you don't want to do the stacking thing, there are very inexpensive second-hand phones with Astro mode, like the Google Pixel 3 onwards.

Seriously, it can't be difficult. I can do it.

---

Stacking is when free programs like Sequator line up multiple photos of the same scene and combine them, keeping real detail while averaging out random noise, so the final image is cleaner and shows more detail. Thank you, Charles.
