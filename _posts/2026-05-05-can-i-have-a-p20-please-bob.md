Afternoon all, your friendly certified idiot, checking in.

Before I started this I had *no idea whatsoever* that incredible pieces of photography could be achieved with kit that can be attained for less than the cost of a hangover. Which is super annoying, given I used to live in Bortle 1. And had many hangovers.

So I'm now taking up the task of trying to get the best shots I can with the most financially accessible gear. Because out there is a guy like me from however many years ago who needs to know that almost any gear will do.

This was just one shot with a Huawei P20. New price: €680. Picked up by me in a second-hand shop for £45. A 30-second exposure, ISO 1000, no editing.

![A wide-field Huawei P20 photo of the Milky Way and stars in the night sky](/assets/images/can-i-have-a-p20-please-bob/huawei-p20-milky-way.jpg)

Not a bad place to start, I reckon.

I would have got more than five lights, but I forgot to focus the camera. I refer you to the opening sentence of this post.

Thanks for coming to my Ted Talk.
