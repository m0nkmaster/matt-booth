To begin almost at the beginning...

As has been said before, on a few occasions in my life I've lived in places with light pollution far, far lower than the norm, and habitually clear skies. If I'd have known shots like this one were possible with pretty much any digital camera and a free laptop program that even the oldest piece of junk can handle, I'd have taken many more shots!

Not bad for a first attempt, eh?!

![Zodiacal light rising above the night sky near the Roque de los Muchachos observatories on La Palma](/assets/images/zodiacal-light/zodiacal-light.webp)

This was captured with far more lights than was necessary on a Samsung S7, propped up against a rock just down the road from the observatories on Roque de los Muchachos on La Palma, during Starmus 2025. I'd had — for reasons only a psychologist would be able to get out of me — a day spent in a terrible mood, made better by both pizza and the crazy drive up to the observatories.

Apparently I'd caught a glimpse of the zodiacal light, which some photographers spend ages trying to get a shot of.

Well, lucky me.

It was also a major part of The Curly One's PhD.

Might just email him the photo.
